# Scaff It — brand guide

Status: selected identity, 2026-11-04. Source menus use the shared palette. Published executables retain their previous appearance until rebuilt and released.

## Positioning
Scaff It helps people set up organised project folders with one shared place for the plan, decisions and progress. Whichever AI assistant they use can work from the same project information.

Audience: people building projects with AI who want their work to stay organised and their AI assistants to follow the same plan.

Main benefit: change AI assistants without changing where your project information lives.

Headline: **One project folder. A clear plan for every AI.**

Supporting copy: Set up organised folders for your project, with one place for the plan, decisions and progress. Give whichever AI assistant you use the same information to work from.

Tagline: **Set up your project. Start building.**

Short description: Organised project folders with a shared plan for your AI assistants.
Attribution: A project setup tool by Kriio Works.

## Visual idea
The selected mark is a folder with a smooth, rising S-shaped ribbon and a small square in the lower-right area. Only the square's bottom-right corner is rounded; its other three corners stay square. The folder gives the project a clear home, the ribbon suggests continuity, and the gold tab adds a recognisable accent. The ribbon and small square are transparent openings, so they adapt to the surface behind the mark. Keep both details in every primary logo version. The board uses a quiet grid, generous space, and plain language.

On the 120-unit master drawing, the square runs from (79,84) to (92,97) with a 4-unit bottom-right radius. The selected version supersedes the earlier blocky S, ribbon-only option, and square-corner variant.

## Colour
| Role | Colour | Hex |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary text, logo, dark surfaces | Ink | #200808 |
| Folder tab, highlights | Gold | #B09050 |
| Primary actions, strong accents | Garnet | #A02838 |
| Main light surface, reversed text | Cream | #F0E8D0 |
| Secondary text on light surfaces | Burgundy | #6A1828 |

Use ink text on gold buttons, and cream text on garnet or burgundy buttons. Gold is an accent on light backgrounds; use ink or burgundy for readable body text. The interactive text menu uses cream on ink, gold headings, garnet action labels, and burgundy section labels. Redirected output and NO_COLOR stay plain. Use the light logo on cream, and the reversed logo on ink. Colour alone must not convey status.

## Interface contrast
Cream on ink: 15.60:1. Cream on burgundy: 9.67:1. Cream on garnet: 6.01:1. Gold on ink: 6.32:1. Use ink text on gold; cream on gold is unsuitable for ordinary text. Colour must accompany readable labels rather than replace them.

## Typography
Segoe UI for headings, interface, and prose. Use Regular for body text and Semibold for headings and actions. Use Cascadia Mono with Consolas as a fallback for commands and paths. Web fallback: Segoe UI, system-ui, sans-serif. These are system-font recommendations; no font software is bundled.

The wordmarks retain editable SVG text and require Segoe UI for the intended appearance. Outline lettering before a final production handoff where the font may be unavailable. The symbol and app icon are pure vector paths.

## Logo use
Keep clear space of at least one fifth of the symbol height around the symbol and one quarter of its height around the full lockup. Start with 32 px minimum for the standalone mark and 140 px width for the wordmark; inspect the actual rendered result. The exported 16 px and 32 px icons show the small-size appearance; the 16 px version retains the geometry but loses fine corner detail. Keep proportions, use approved colours, and keep the symbol upright. Do not remove the small square or round its other corners. Use the monochrome version where colour is unavailable. The transparent reversed wordmark needs a dark background.

## Voice
Precise, approachable, and easy to understand. Lead with organised project folders and shared project information. Explain the benefit before the details. Use short sentences and everyday words. Explain what the user can review and choose.

Say "AI assistant" instead of "agent", "project notes" instead of "context", "shared project information" instead of "source of truth", and "set up your project folder" instead of "scaffold your repository". Keep specialised language out of public-facing headlines and explanations.

Scaff It creates the starting folders and guidance files. People and their AI assistants must keep the project notes current and use them. Do not imply that Scaff It connects AI services, updates notes automatically, or guarantees that every AI will read or follow them. It does not generate a finished application.

- Welcome: Give your AI a clear place to start.
- Preview: Review your files. Create when ready.
- Success: Your project folder is ready. Start building.
- Existing project: Organise an existing project.
- Download: Download for Windows.

The brand-board interface and download card are concept applications, not screenshots of the shipping product.

## Files

systems. `assets/scaff-it-project-preview.svg` and `.png` show an illustrative
excerpt of the generated folder layout, not a screenshot of the app.
