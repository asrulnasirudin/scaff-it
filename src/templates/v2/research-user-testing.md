# User testing (sanitized)

Only **sanitized** material belongs here: protocols, findings and reports with no names, faces, voices, contact details or other identifying information.

- `protocols/`: what will be tested and how.
- `findings/`: sanitized observations.
- `reports/`: summaries for decisions.

Raw recordings, notes, participant data and consent forms live only in `local/user-testing/` (git-ignored). Never copy them here.
